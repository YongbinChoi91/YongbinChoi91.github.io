Automatic differentiation and parallel execution

Weak-form models, Sacado AD, and parallel-ready assembly.

HiPaCs combines weak-form-driven model definition with Trilinos Sacado automatic differentiation and OpenMP/MPI-ready assembly paths. Local element and RVE contributions are converted into residuals and tangents, scattered into a global CSR system, and solved through interchangeable sparse linear solver backends.

Local weak form G model-level physics
Trilinos Sacado AD residual + tangent
OpenMP / MPI assembly element and RVE loops
Global CSR + solver UMFPACK / PETSc / Pardiso
