Design point

  • Supports T3/T6, Q4/Q9, Tet4/Tet10, H8/H20/H27, and Polygon.
  • Keeps boundary-edge topology consistent with assembly and postprocessing.
  • Uses exact polynomial Hessian path where needed for stabilization.
